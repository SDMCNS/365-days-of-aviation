## 2. Institutional Roles & Interfaces
A flight operates at the intersection of regulated entities:
- **Airlines (AOC Holders)**: Responsible for the operational control, airworthiness acceptance, crew scheduling, and passenger service.
- **Airports (ADR Operators)**: Provide physical movement surfaces, obstacle clearance, rescue and firefighting (RFFS), and gate infrastructure.
- **Air Navigation Service Providers (ANSPs)**: Ensure safe separation, flow management, and flight efficiency across controlled airspace.
- **Regulators (ICAO / EASA / NAAs)**: Establish safety rules, certify organisations, and conduct ongoing safety oversight.
