## Slide 2: Tier 1: Global Public International Law (ICAO)
*Constitutional Foundation of Civil Aviation Under the Chicago Convention* — **[Global Governance]**

> **Key Takeaway**: ICAO establishes global airspace sovereignty and baseline safety standards through the Chicago Convention and its 19 Annexes.

- Chicago Convention (Doc 7300): Article 1 grants complete and exclusive sovereignty over airspace; Article 5 governs non-scheduled flights.
- 19 ICAO Annexes: Contain SARPs where Standards ('shall') mandate compliance and Recommended Practices ('should') guide desirable behavior.
- PANS Series & Global Plans: PANS-OPS (Doc 8168), PANS-ATM (Doc 4444), GASP (Safety), and GANP (Modernization/ASBU Blocks).
- USOAP Continuous Monitoring: Evaluates state compliance across 8 Critical Elements of safety oversight.

```text
TECHNICAL: State differences to SARPs must be formally filed under Article 38 and published in national AIP GEN 1.7 sections.
```

*Presenter Script*: At the apex of the aviation pyramid is Tier 1, anchored by the 1944 Chicago Convention. It grants nations absolute sovereignty over their airspace while creating the framework for global technical harmonization via SARPs.

## Slide 3: Tier 2: Supranational Law (European Union & EASA)
*Direct Applicability and The 7 Pillars of European Aviation Safety* — **[Supranational Regulation]**

> **Key Takeaway**: EASA exercises centralized competence across 27 member states through Binding Regulations and Easy Access Rules.

- TFEU Article 288: EU Regulations possess direct applicability and legal primacy over national laws across all member states.
- Basic Regulation (EU) 2018/1139: Grants EASA direct competence over Type Certification, Design Organisation Approvals (DOA), and Third-Country Operators.
- The 7 EASA Domains: Encompassing Part-21, Part-M/CAMO/145, Part-CAT/SPA/NCC/NCO, Part-FCL, Part-ADR, ATM/ANS (Reg 2017/373), and Drones.
- Easy Access Rules (EAR): Integrates Hard Law (Implementing Rules) with Soft Law (Certification Specifications, AMC, Guidance Material).

```text
TECHNICAL: Acceptable Means of Compliance (AMC) provide a legal presumption of conformity when implemented by certified organizations.
```

*Presenter Script*: In Europe, Tier 2 supranational legislation supersedes national rules via TFEU Article 288. EASA governs the entire aviation domain through structured Easy Access Rules that clearly separate hard law from compliance means.

## Slide 4: Tiers 3 & 4: National Authorities and Certified Operators
*Translating Regulations into Airline Operations, Maintenance, and Airspace* — **[State & Operator Oversight]**

> **Key Takeaway**: National Aviation Authorities issue operating certificates while airlines and MROs execute compliance via postholders.

- Tier 3 (NAAs): National authorities (CAA/DGAC) issue AOCs, pilot licenses (FCL), aerodrome certificates, and oversee State Safety Programmes (SSP).
- AIP Integration: Enforces local constraints including night curfews (e.g., EDDF), noise abatement procedures, and specific glidepath angles (e.g., LCY 5.5°).
- Tier 4 (Operators): Airlines operate under Part-CAT/ORO led by an Accountable Manager (ORO.GEN.210) and mandatory nominated postholders.
- Maintenance & Infrastructure: Part-CAMO manages continuous airworthiness while Part-145 MROs execute heavy maintenance and line servicing.

```text
TECHNICAL: Enterprise Regulatory Registers (ORO.GEN.200) link every operational procedure directly back to statutory legal mandates.
```

*Presenter Script*: Moving down to Tiers 3 and 4, National Authorities act as the bridge between international rules and local operations. Airlines and maintenance organizations operationalize these standards through strict management hierarchies.

## Slide 5: Tier 5: Closed-Loop Traceability & Frontline Proof
*The Quad-Link Architecture and Indestructible Operational Evidence* — **[Frontline Execution]**

> **Key Takeaway**: Safety and compliance are proven through closed-loop data linkages from statutory rules to frontline flight logs.

- The Quad-Link Architecture: Connects Statutory Rule -> Responsible Postholder -> Frontline SOP -> Safety Performance Indicator -> Indestructible Proof.
- Technical Log & CRS (Part-145.A.50): Every maintenance action concludes with a signed Certificate of Release to Service in the aircraft tech log.
- Operational Flight Planning: Fuel policy execution governed by Commission Regulation (EU) 2021/1296 ensuring mandatory reserves.
- Flight Data Monitoring (FDM): Continuous black-box telemetry analysis feeding proactive safety management systems (SMS).

```text
TECHNICAL: Traceability formula ensures zero regulatory orphans: every frontline task points to an SOP, which points to a postholder, which points to a rule.
```

*Presenter Script*: Tier 5 represents the tangible proof of compliance. Through the Quad-Link architecture, every flight plan, tech log entry, and FDM exceedance report provides indisputable evidence that the entire 5-tier ecosystem is functioning correctly.
