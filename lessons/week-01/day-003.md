## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Build Objective**: Create a concrete operational system map showing how standardized digital information flows tie the aviation ecosystem together:

1. **Aeronautical Telecommunication Networks**: AFTN and AMHS store-and-forward international routing.
2. **The ICAO Flight Plan (FPL)**: Standardized format (ICAO Doc 4444) conveying aircraft type, wake category, PBN navigation capability, route, alternate, and endurance.
3. **Statutory Energy Balance**: How EASA Part-CAT / ICAO Annex 6 mathematically mandates Ramp Fuel = Taxi + Trip + 5% Contingency + Alternate + 30-minute Final Reserve.
4. **Air Traffic Flow Management (ATFM)**: Filing deadlines (EOBT - 180 min), delay updates (DLA), and slot allocation messages (SAM/CTOT).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: An airline dispatcher attempts to release an aircraft with fuel below the statutory alternate + final reserve minimum to accommodate excess commercial cargo.
- **Cascading Ripple**: Automated IFPS / OCC validation blocks the flight plan with a rejection (`REJ`), grounding the departure until certified fuel reserves are loaded.
- **Risk Mitigation**: The statutory 30-minute Final Reserve Fuel is legally inviolable; breaching it in flight requires declaring an immediate "MAYDAY FUEL".
