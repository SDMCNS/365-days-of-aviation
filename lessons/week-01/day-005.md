## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Apply Objective**: Trace how an airline, airport, and air navigation system collaboratively move an aircraft and its passengers across 12 discrete operational milestones.

Today's lesson breaks down the five temporal horizons:
1. **Commercial & Strategic (D-365 to D-30)**: PNR reservation generation, Global Distribution Systems (GDS), and IATA Level 3 Airport Slot allocation.
2. **Tactical Planning (D-30 to D-1)**: EASA Part-ORO.FTL crew rostering, aircraft maintenance cycles (Part-M), and Certificate of Release to Service (CRS).
3. **Operational Dispatch (D-3h to D-45m)**: Operational Flight Plan (OFP) generation, statutory fuel planning (Trip + 5% Contingency + Alternate + 30m Final Reserve), and Eurocontrol IFPS Flight Plan acceptance.
4. **Ground Turnaround (D-45m to D-0)**: Positive passenger-baggage reconciliation (ICAO Annex 17), electronic loadsheet/trim calculation (CG envelope limits), and A-CDM pre-departure clearance.
5. **Airborne Trajectory & Arrival (D-0 to In-Blocks)**: SID departure climb, RVSM en-route separation, Top of Descent (TOD) 3:1 idle profile calculation, and Aircraft Technical Log closing.

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode 1 (Gate No-Show)**: Boarded passenger fails to appear at gate. Bag in cargo hold must be identified and offloaded under ICAO Annex 17, missing Target Startup Time (TSAT).
- **Failure Mode 2 (Jet Stream Headwind Surge)**: 75-knot headwind increases flight time and burns contingency fuel. Flight crew must monitor remaining fuel against Alternate + Final Reserve to avoid emergency declaration.
- **Failure Mode 3 (Trim Sheet Discrepancy)**: Cargo loaded in incorrect hold moves Center of Gravity outside certified forward limit, risking aerodynamic pitch control failure on rotation.
