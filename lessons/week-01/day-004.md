## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Challenge Objective**: Dissect the serviceable aircraft paradox: why physical airworthiness is merely one gate among six independent non-compensatory operational requirements:

1. **Flight Crew Fatigue & Duty Limitations (ORO.FTL)**: Mandatory maximum Flight Duty Periods (FDP). When cumulative ground delay causes projected duty to exceed legal limits by even 1 minute, the crew is grounded.
2. **Aerodrome Operating Minima & Runway Friction (Part-CAT & GRF)**: Runway Visual Range (RVR) below certified minimums, or contaminated runway friction (RWYCC 1) exceeding aircraft crosswind limits.
3. **Noise Curfews & ATFM Slots**: Strict municipal night flight bans (e.g. Frankfurt 23:00 curfew) where late arrivals are denied landing rights regardless of aircraft status.
4. **Airspace Sovereignty & Diplomatic Overflight**: Sovereign FIR closures or permit revocations rendering the route impassable.
5. **Aerodrome Rescue & Firefighting Services (RFFS)**: Airport fire category downgrades below the aircraft's certified minimum (e.g. A320 requires Category 6).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: Destination aerodrome foam crash tender suffers mechanical failure, downgrading RFFS from Category 7 to Category 4.
- **Cascading Ripple**: Commercial passenger flights on narrowbody and widebody airliners are legally barred from landing; airborne flights must divert and pending departures are cancelled.
- **Risk Mitigation**: Airline Operations Control (OCC) continuous NOTAM monitoring and pre-dispatch aerodrome status verification.
