## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Derive Objective**: Map the operational boundaries, legal duties, and real-time handshakes between the 7 key stakeholders of civil aviation:

1. **Airlines (AOC Holders)**: Operational control, flight planning, crew scheduling under Flight Time Limitations (FTL), airworthiness acceptance, and commercial flight execution.
2. **Aerodrome Operators (ADR)**: Physical runways, taxiways, apron stands, obstacle clearance, rescue and firefighting (RFFS), and surface friction monitoring (Global Reporting Format - GRF).
3. **Air Navigation Service Providers (ANSPs)**: Air traffic control (ATC), terminal/en-route separation, airspace structure, and Air Traffic Flow Management (ATFM) slots.
4. **Ground Handling Agents (SGHA)**: Ramp servicing, aircraft fueling (into-plane), passenger boarding, baggage security reconciliation, pushback, and de-icing.
5. **Continuing Airworthiness Management (CAMO / Part-145)**: Ongoing technical maintenance, Airworthiness Directives (ADs), and release to service.
6. **Aircraft & Engine Manufacturers (Type Certificate Holders / Part-21)**: Aircraft Flight Manual (AFM), Master Minimum Equipment List (MMEL), and structural integrity.
7. **Regulators (ICAO / EASA / National Aviation Authorities)**: Standards setting (SARPs), initial organization approvals, and ongoing safety oversight.

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: A checked passenger fails to board at final gate call (no-show).
- **Cascading Ripple**: Under ICAO Annex 17 security rules, the aircraft cannot depart with unaccompanied baggage. Reopening the cargo hold to identify and offload the bag burns 12–15 minutes, risking forfeiture of the Eurocontrol ATFM Calculated Take-Off Time (CTOT) slot and causing a 45–90 minute ground hold delay.
- **Risk Mitigation**: Airport Collaborative Decision Making (A-CDM) milestones (Target Off-Block Time - TOBT, Target Start-up Approval Time - TSAT).
