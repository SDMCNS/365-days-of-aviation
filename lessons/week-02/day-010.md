## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Build Objective**: Construct a rigorous 5-tier hierarchy map connecting high-level international treaty law to technical SARPs, operational procedures, and cockpit checklists.

Today's lesson builds:
1. **The 5 Tiers of Aviation Authority**:
   - *Tier 1 (Treaty)*: Chicago Convention Doc 7300 (supreme sovereign treaty).
   - *Tier 2 (SARPs)*: 19 ICAO Annexes adopted by Council.
   - *Tier 3 (PANS)*: Worldwide operational procedures (Doc 4444 PANS-ATM, Doc 8168 PANS-OPS).
   - *Tier 4 (Guidance)*: Technical manuals and circulars (Doc 9859 SMS, Doc 9976 Fuel).
   - *Tier 5 (Enacted Law)*: Sovereign regulations (EASA/FAA) and company manuals (OM-A/B/C/D).
2. **Hierarchy Precedence & Conflict Resolution**: Why a lower-tier document can never relax a higher-tier requirement, and how legal conflicts are adjudicated.
3. **Traceability Chains**: Tracing operational mandates (e.g. 30-min final reserve fuel, maintenance release, runway friction) from Chicago Convention articles down to cockpit execution.

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode 1 (Broken Traceability)**: An airline introduces an unapproved dispatch procedure that lacks regulatory backing from national law, resulting in immediate AOC suspension.
- **Failure Mode 2 (Conflicting Manual Precedence)**: A company manual relaxes fuel or crew duty limits below statutory EASA/ICAO standards. A SAFA ramp check issues a Level 1 non-compliance finding and grounds the aircraft.
- **Failure Mode 3 (Guidance Treated as Law)**: Confusing non-binding advisory material (Tier 4) with mandatory SARPs (Tier 2), leading to unnecessary operational cancellations or missed regulatory baselines.

## 5. Regulatory Evidence Record
- **ICAO Doc 7300, Articles 37, 54(l)**: Council authority to adopt Annexes.
- **ICAO Directives to Regional Air Navigation Meetings**: PANS approval and scope.
- **EASA Part-ARO.GEN.350**: Findings and corrective action mechanisms for non-compliance.
