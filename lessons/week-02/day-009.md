## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Derive Objective**: Mathematically and procedurally analyze how global aviation achieves safety standardization through Standards ("shall") vs Recommended Practices ("should"), and how national differences are filed under Article 38.

Today's lesson breaks down:
1. **The Legal Distinction**:
   - **Standards**: Mandatory baselines necessary for the safety or regularity of international air navigation. States must conform or file an immediate Article 38 difference.
   - **Recommended Practices**: Desirable specifications where compliance is voluntary/advisory.
2. **The Article 38 Safety Valve**: Why international law permits sovereign deviations, and how differences are classified:
   - *Category A*: More exacting (stricter safety margin).
   - *Category B*: Different character / equivalent alternative means.
   - *Category C*: Less protective / sub-standard (creates market access liabilities).
3. **The Article 33 Consequence**: Why mutual recognition of airworthiness and licenses is conditional: if a State files a Category C difference below ICAO baseline, foreign nations are legally entitled to ban that State's airlines from their skies.
4. **Where Differences Live**: Section **GEN 1.7** of every national Aeronautical Information Publication (AIP).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode 1 (Unnotified Difference)**: A State modifies an operating rule without notifying ICAO under Article 38, breaching international treaty obligations and risking immediate aircraft detentions.
- **Failure Mode 2 (Sub-Standard Category C Exceedance)**: A nation relaxes fuel reserve standards or maintenance check cycles below ICAO baselines. Foreign regulators revoke Third Country Operator (TCO) permits under Article 33, grounding international routes.
- **Failure Mode 3 (Altimetry & Units Confusion)**: Failing to account for notified national differences (e.g. US inHg vs European hPa pressure settings) causes altitude deviations and CFIT (Controlled Flight Into Terrain) risks.
