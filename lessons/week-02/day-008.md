## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Understand Objective**: Grasp why complete national airspace sovereignty (Article 1) defines every route, flight plan, overflight fee, and international airline operation.

Today's lesson analyzes:
1. **The Chicago Convention (1944)**: How 54 nations established the global charter for safe, regular, and orderly air transport across sovereign borders.
2. **Article 1 Sovereignty**: Complete and exclusive sovereignty over airspace above territorial land and waters (12 NM), contrasting with the High Seas (Article 12) where ICAO Annex 2 rules apply without exception.
3. **The Role of ICAO**: Why ICAO is a standard-setting UN agency in Montreal rather than an operational police force, and why international standards (SARPs) require State transposition into national law.
4. **Scheduled vs Non-Scheduled Flights**: The operational and legal distinction between Article 5 (transit rights for non-scheduled flights) and Article 6 (bilateral agreements required for scheduled commercial airlines).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode 1 (Airspace Violation)**: Entering sovereign airspace without diplomatic clearance or valid flight plan filing risks military interception under ICAO Annex 2 (Rules of the Air).
- **Failure Mode 2 (Geopolitical Airspace Closure)**: Sovereign closure of key transit airspace (e.g. Siberian corridor or Middle East) forces detours of +1,450 NM, adding €27,000 in fuel and airframe cost per flight and threatening fuel exhaustion.
- **Failure Mode 3 (Technical En-Route Diversion)**: Detour burn forces an unscheduled intermediate landing in a third country, triggering uncoordinated ramp handling, landing fees, and missed passenger connections.
