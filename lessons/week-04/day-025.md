## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Challenge Objective**: Deconstructing the 4 Pathologies of Flawed Regulatory Material.

Operating on flawed, outdated, or misapplied regulatory texts creates grave operational, financial, and legal perils:
1. **Pathology 1: Obsolete / Revoked Legislation**: Citing repealed frameworks (e.g., JAR-OPS 1 or national rules superseded by EU law).
2. **Pathology 2: Superseded Amendments within Active Rules**: Relying on parent regulations without subsequent amending acts (e.g. using pre-2022 fuel rules instead of Reg (EU) 2021/1296).
3. **Pathology 3: Unofficial & Third-Party Compilations**: Depending on blogs, unverified slide decks, or non-authentic PDF downloads rather than the Eur-Lex Official Journal.
4. **Pathology 4: Out-of-Context Jurisdictional Cross-Contamination**: Applying FAA FAR Part 121 domestic rules to an EASA Part-CAT aircraft or vice versa.

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: Dispatching an international passenger flight using an out-of-context domestic fuel rule that omits destination alternate fuel.
- **Cascading Hazard**: Severe fuel shortfall upon arrival. In the event of destination weather degradation below minima, the aircraft risks fuel exhaustion or emergency diversion.
- **Enforcement Action**: EC SAFA ramp inspection Class 3 Finding, immediate aircraft detention, potential pilot license suspension, and repudiation of insurance coverage under standard policy clauses.
