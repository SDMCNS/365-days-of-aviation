## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Derive Objective**: Deriving the governing EASA regulatory domain and rulebook for any aviation operation.

European aviation law is structured across 7 dedicated regulatory domains:
1. **Initial Airworthiness (Part-21 / Reg 748/2012)**: Type certification, DOA, POA, modifications.
2. **Continuing Airworthiness (Part-M / Part-145 / Part-CAMO / Reg 1321/2014)**: Maintenance and airworthiness management.
3. **Air Operations (Air Ops / Reg 965/2012)**: Part-CAT, Part-SPA, Part-NCC, Part-NCO, Part-SPO.
4. **Aircrew & Licensing (Reg 1178/2011)**: Part-FCL, Part-MED, Part-CC, Part-ATO.
5. **Aerodromes & Ground Operations (Part-ADR / Reg 139/2014)**: Airport safety, RFFS, GRF.
6. **Air Traffic Management (ATM/ANS / Reg 2017/373)**: ATS, CNS, MET, AIS providers.
7. **Unmanned Aircraft Systems (Reg 2019/947 & 2019/945)**: Open, Specific, and Certified UAS categories.

Understanding the internal anatomy of **Easy Access Rules (EAR)** separates Hard Law (Implementing Rules) from Soft Law (Certification Specifications, AMC, and GM).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: Operational misclassification — attempting to fly commercial charter passengers or aerial survey operations under private rules (`Part-NCO`).
- **Cascading Hazard**: Complete absence of required organisational oversight (no Part-ORO management system, no mandatory training, no approved CAMO). In the event of an incident, insurance coverage is legally voided.
- **Enforcement Action**: Level 1 non-compliance finding under `Part-ARO.GEN.350`, immediate SAFA ramp grounding, and potential criminal referral for operating without an Air Operator Certificate (AOC).

## 5. Regulatory Evidence Record
- **Governing Body**: European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) & National Aviation Authorities.
- **Statutory Anchor**: Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 Article 30; Regulation (EU) No 965/2012; ORO.GEN.120 (AltMoC).
- **Applicability**: Aircraft Operators (CAT, NCC, NCO, SPO), MROs, Design Teams, Flight Crews, Aerodrome Operators.
- **Compliance Demonstration**: Operations Manual (OM Parts A-D), Continuous Airworthiness Management Exposition (CAME), MOE, and AltMoC approvals.
