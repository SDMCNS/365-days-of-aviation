## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Build Objective**: Constructing a Controlled Enterprise Regulatory Source Register.

Under EASA regulations, every air operator (AOC), maintenance organisation (Part-145), and certified aerodrome must maintain a master inventory of all statutory instruments governing its activities. The 6 core attributes of a controlled entry are:
1. **Document Title**: Official title of the legislative act.
2. **Legal Identifier**: Precise statutory reference (e.g., `Reg (EU) No 965/2012 Annex IV`).
3. **Version & Amendment State**: Latest amending regulation in the Official Journal (e.g., `Reg (EU) 2021/1296`).
4. **Effective & Transition Dates**: Entry into force and mandatory enforcement deadlines.
5. **Regulatory Status**: Active, In-Transition, Superseded, or Revoked.
6. **Operational Scope & Internal Link**: Target aircraft fleets, company manual chapters (OM-A, CAME, MOE), and assigned Nominated Persons.

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: Regulatory currency lag — operating under superseded rules past the mandatory transition deadline (e.g. failing to adopt the EASA 2021/1296 Fuel Scheme).
- **Cascading Hazard**: Flight dispatch calculations fail to meet statutory legal baselines; pilot manuals contradict European law.
- **Enforcement Action**: Level 1 non-compliance finding under `Part-ARO.GEN.350`, immediate grounding of affected fleet, and invalidation of flight dispatch releases.
