## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Integrate Objective**: Constructing the 5-Node Traceability Architecture.

Under Performance-Based Regulation (PBR), a regulatory rule must be woven into an unbreakable 5-node chain:
1. **Node 1: Statutory Requirement (The Rule)**: Primary law, IR, or CS (e.g., `CAT.POL.A.205` / `CS-25.107` Take-off Net Flight Path $\ge 35\text{ ft}$).
2. **Node 2: Responsible Organisation & Postholder**: Named executive held legally accountable (e.g., Nominated Person Flight Operations).
3. **Node 3: Operational Process (The SOP)**: Approved procedure executed by crews (e.g. Cockpit Electronic Flight Bag Takeoff calculation).
4. **Node 4: Safety Objective (SPI / SPT)**: Measurable target under SMS (e.g., zero runway overruns; tailstrike rate $< 0.05$ per 10,000 departures).
5. **Node 5: Audit Evidence Record (The Proof)**: Immutable verification record (e.g., archived EFB logs, FDM flight recorder data, Technical Log CRS).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: Unrecorded maintenance action — replacing an elevator servo actuator without generating a signed Certificate of Release to Service (CRS) in the Aircraft Technical Log.
- **Cascading Hazard**: Immediate invalidation of the aircraft's Certificate of Airworthiness (CofA) under `Part-M.A.801`.
- **Enforcement Action**: Level 1 non-compliance finding served by NAA, instant grounding of the aircraft on stand, and mandatory duplicate inspection before flight release.

---

## 5. Regulatory Evidence Record
- **Governing Body**: ICAO, EASA, and National Aviation Authorities.
- **Statutory Anchor**: ICAO Annex 19 Chapter 4; Regulation (EU) No 965/2012 ORO.GEN.200; Part-145.A.50.
- **Applicability**: Aircraft Operators, Maintenance Organisations (MROs), CAMOs, Ground Handlers.
- **Compliance Demonstration**: Operations Manual (OM-A/B), CAME, MOE, Safety Performance Indicators (SPIs), and Audited Tech Log Records.
