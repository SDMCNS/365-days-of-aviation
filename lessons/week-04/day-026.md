## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Apply Objective**: Executing the 4-Step Primary Source Legal Research Protocol.

Aviation safety requires tracing operational rules back to enacted primary law rather than secondary summaries:
1. **Locate Enacted Instrument**: Commission Regulation (EU) No 965/2012 Annex IV (`CAT.GEN.MPA.140` PEDs & `CAT.GEN.MPA.200` Dangerous Goods).
2. **Cross-Reference Soft Law**: EASA ED Decisions (`AMC1 CAT.GEN.MPA.140` EMI certification & `AMC2` cabin crew firefighting).
3. **Intersect Technical Standards**: ICAO Doc 9284 (Technical Instructions) & EASA SIB 2017-01R1 (Smart Bags).
4. **Define Operational Boundaries**: Exactly who must comply, permitted Watt-hour battery thresholds, and why spare power banks are banned in checked baggage (Halon ineffectiveness vs water cooling).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: A passenger checks a smart suitcase with an integrated, non-removable $100\text{ Wh}$ battery into the lower cargo hold.
- **Cascading Hazard**: Mechanical crush triggers thermal runaway at FL370. Halon 1301 extinguishes open flames but cannot cool the internal cell reaction ($>600^\circ\text{C}$), leading to thermal propagation, toxic gas release, and an in-flight cargo fire emergency.
- **Active Barrier**: Automated check-in/gate baggage profiling, mandatory battery removal, and passenger cabin containment kits.
