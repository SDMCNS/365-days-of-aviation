## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Understand Objective**: Deconstructing the 6 Tiers of the ICAO Global Regulatory Library.

In international civil aviation, documents published by ICAO carry vastly different legal weight and operational enforceability:
1. **Tier 1 — The Convention (Doc 7300)**: Constitutional treaty ratified by 193 member States.
2. **Tier 2 — The 19 Annexes (SARPs)**: Standards ("shall") and Recommended Practices ("should") adopted by 2/3 vote of the ICAO Council.
3. **Tier 3 — Procedures for Air Navigation Services (PANS)**: Council-approved operational rules (PANS-ATM Doc 4444, PANS-OPS Doc 8168, PANS-TRG Doc 9868, PANS-ADR Doc 9981).
4. **Tier 4 — Technical Guidance Manuals**: Secretary General publications (Doc 9859 SMM, Doc 9613 PBN, Doc 9365 AWO) providing "how-to" implementation engineering.
5. **Tier 5 — Circulars**: Informational studies and regional exploratory reports (e.g. Cir 328 on UAS).
6. **Tier 6 — Global Strategic Plans**: Assembly policy roadmaps (GASP Doc 10004, GANP Doc 9750).

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Failure Mode**: An unnotified construction crane or antenna is erected inside the intermediate or final instrument approach corridor.
- **Cascading Hazard**: Loss of the required 150 m / 75 m Minimum Obstacle Clearance (MOC). If terrain relief surcharge is omitted, aircraft operating in turbulence or wind shear face Controlled Flight Into Terrain (CFIT) risk.
- **Active Barrier**: Immediate NOTAM issuance, procedure altitude amendment, or approach plate suspension under ICAO Annex 14 / PANS-OPS safeguarding rules.

## 5. Regulatory Evidence Record
- **Governing Body**: ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization).
- **Statutory Anchor**: Articles 37, 54, and 90 of the Chicago Convention; PANS-OPS Doc 8168 Vol II.
- **Applicability**: Air Navigation Service Providers (ANSPs), Procedure Designers, Flight Operations, State Civil Aviation Authorities.
- **Compliance Demonstration**: Published AIP Instrument Approach Charts, State Differences Notification (AIP GEN 1.7), and Obstacle Assessment Surface (OAS) survey records.
