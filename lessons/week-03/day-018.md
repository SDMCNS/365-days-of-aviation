## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Challenge Objective**: Distinguish a legal obligation from AMC, GM, a certification specification and an internal company procedure.

Not all regulatory text is created equal. European civil aviation operates under a multi-tier normative hierarchy:
1. **Hard Law (Implementing Regulations)**: Binding in its entirety. Absolute statutory force. Direct applicability under Article 288 TFEU. Zero deviations permitted without Commission Article 71 exemption.
2. **Certification Specifications (CS)**: Technical standards that become binding once selected as the Type Certification Basis or AOC baseline.
3. **Acceptable Means of Compliance (AMC)**: Soft law issued by EASA. Adhering to the AMC grants an automatic **Presumption of Conformity**. Operators are legally entitled to propose an **Alternative Means of Compliance (AltMoC)** under `ORO.GEN.120` if safety equivalence is proven.
4. **Guidance Material (GM)**: Purely explanatory. **An auditor cannot legally issue an audit finding citing only a GM!**
5. **Company Procedures (SOPs / OM)**: Accepted/approved by the National Competent Authority as part of the AOC. Legally binding on internal flight crew under corporate operating mandates.

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Scenario**: An over-zealous regulatory inspector issues a formal Level 2 audit finding against an airline, citing only a non-binding suggestion in Guidance Material (`GM1 ORO.GEN.200`).
- **Administrative Defense**: The airline demonstrates that it complies with the underlying Implementing Rule through its approved SMS manual. Because Guidance Material is purely advisory and non-enforceable, the legal finding is quashed and expunged from the operator's record.
