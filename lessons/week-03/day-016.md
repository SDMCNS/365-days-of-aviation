## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Derive Objective**: The relationship between EU regulations, national implementation, oversight and organisational compliance.

Under **Article 288 TFEU**, EU regulations are directly applicable: they enter national law immediately without domestic legislation. The operational translation occurs through two paired rulebooks:
1. **Authority Requirements (Part-ARO)**: Directs the National Competent Authority on how to conduct continuous surveillance (ARO.GEN.300), establish risk-based oversight planning cycles of 12 to 48 months (ARO.GEN.305), and manage enforcement findings (ARO.GEN.350).
2. **Organisation Requirements (Part-ORO)**: Directs the airline on internal management structure (ORO.GEN.200), appointing a single **Accountable Manager** (ORO.GEN.210) with financial and legal liability, and managing compliance monitoring systems (CMS).
3. **The Finding Lifecycle**:
   - **Level 1 Finding**: Lowers or seriously hazards flight safety $\implies$ immediate AOC restriction or suspension.
   - **Level 2 Finding**: Non-compliance which could lower safety $\implies$ 90-day corrective action clock.
   - **Escalation**: Failing to rectify a Level 2 finding within 90 days results in mandatory escalation to **Level 1**.

## 4. Break It / Stress Test & Safety Hazards
- **Scenario**: An airline ignores an authority finding regarding crew fatigue software anomalies past the statutory 90-day closure window.
- **Regulatory Escalation**: Under **ARO.GEN.350(e)**, the Level 2 finding automatically escalates to a **Level 1 Finding**. The authority immediately restricts the airline's Operations Specifications, capping daily flight sectors and triggering widespread flight cancellations and direct passenger delay liability under Regulation (EC) 261/2004.
