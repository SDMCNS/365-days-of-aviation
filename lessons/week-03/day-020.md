## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Integrate Objective**: Compare international standards, European legislation and local operating arrangements.

A commercial flight is never governed by a single rulebook. It operates at the simultaneous intersection of three distinct governance tiers:
1. **Tier 1: Global Baseline (ICAO)**: High-level diplomatic standards (Annexes 1 to 19) establishing baseline airworthiness, personnel licensing, and environmental noise standards.
2. **Tier 2: Regional Harmonisation (EASA / EU)**: Directly applicable hard law (Regulation (EU) 965/2012) creating a single rulebook for flight operations (Part-CAT), crew duty limits, and runway performance safety factors ($1.67\times$ dry factor).
3. **Tier 3: Local Operating Arrangements (AIP Section AD 2)**: Site-specific operational constraints established by local topography, obstacle clearance, and municipal environmental bylaws:
   - **Night Flight Curfews**: e.g., Frankfurt's total ban on flights between 23:00 and 05:00 local.
   - **Steep Approaches**: e.g., London City's non-standard 5.5° glidepath requiring specialized fly-by-wire approval (`SPA.SPA.100`).
   - **Weekend Closures**: e.g., London City closing strictly at Saturday 12:30 UTC.
