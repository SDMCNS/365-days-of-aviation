## 2. Core Operational & Technical Concept
**Integrate Objective**: Connect passenger systems, airline operations, border processes, security and ground handling.

In the aviation system, no single component operates in isolation. Today's lesson analyzes:
1. **System Governance**: How ICAO Annex 9 (Facilitation), IATA ADRM (Airport Development Reference Manual) establishes mandatory baselines versus recommended practices.
2. **Operational Reality**: How airlines, aerodromes, and air traffic service providers coordinate resources and information in real-time.
3. **Cross-Domain Dependencies**: How technical airworthiness, crew status, weather, and airspace capacity combine to enable or restrict safe operations.
